YouTube Shorts vs long videos: which should your business make?

By Brijaditya SharmaPublished 24 September 20263 min read

Shorts can reach new people fast. Long videos build trust and answer real questions. Most businesses do best with a simple mix of both.

The key difference

Shorts are vertical videos of up to a few minutes, shown in a swipeable feed. They are built for discovery: people who have never heard of you may see them. Long videos are traditional YouTube videos. People often find them through search and suggested videos, and they give you time to explain, demonstrate and build trust.

What Shorts are good for

  • Reaching new viewers quickly.
  • Quick tips, before-and-afters, reactions and teasers.
  • Testing topics before making a longer video.
  • Showing personality in a low-effort format.

The trade-off: viewers swipe quickly, and a Short alone rarely explains enough for someone to hire you.

What long videos are good for

  • Answering the questions your customers search for (“how to…”, “what is…”, “X vs Y”).
  • Showing your expertise in depth — people feel they know you after watching.
  • Building a library that keeps getting found for months or years.

The trade-off: they take more planning, recording and editing.

The simple strategy: use both, from one recording

  1. Record one useful long video around a question your customers ask.
  2. Clip three to five Shorts from its best moments.
  3. Link each Short to the full video using YouTube’s related-video feature where available.
  4. Link the long video to your website in the description and, where it fits, verbally.

This way, Shorts bring new people in, the long video builds trust, and your website turns interest into enquiries.

Tips for better Shorts

  • Start with the most interesting line — no slow introductions.
  • One idea per Short.
  • Add accurate captions.
  • End with a reason to watch the full video or visit your channel.

Tips for better long videos

  • Choose titles that match what people actually search for.
  • Deliver on the title quickly — explain what the viewer will learn early on.
  • Use chapters so people can jump to what they need.
  • Design a clear, readable thumbnail.

What to measure

For Shorts, watch how many people keep watching and how many visit your channel. For long videos, look at average view duration, click-through rate on thumbnails and clicks to your website. YouTube Studio shows all of these.

Quick answers

Common follow-up questions.

Still unsure? Ask me anything or message me on WhatsApp.

Do Shorts help my long videos?

They can, by bringing new viewers to your channel. Link them to related long videos to guide people deeper.

Can I upload TikToks or Reels as Shorts?

You can reuse your own videos, but remove other platforms’ watermarks and adapt captions for YouTube.

How long should a long video be?

As long as it takes to answer the topic well. Cut anything that doesn’t help the viewer.
